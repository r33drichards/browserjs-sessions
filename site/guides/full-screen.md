# Full screen and resize

## Full screen

Choose **Full screen** above the screen. To leave, press `Esc` or choose
**Exit full screen**.

The button is missing where the browser you are using cannot do full screen,
such as on an iPhone.

## Resize

The remote desktop follows the size of the screen box. Make your window
larger or smaller, or go full screen, and the session's browser gets the new
size after a short pause.

The desktop is at most 2560 by 1600. In a larger box the picture is scaled
up to fit.
