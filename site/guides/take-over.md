# Watch and take over

The agent and you use the same desktop. There is one screen.

## Watch

Open the session's page while the agent works. Choose **Full screen** for
the whole display. The desktop takes the size of your window, up to 2560 by
1600.

## Take over

Click into the screen and use your mouse and keyboard. There is no lock and
no hand-over step.

Typical reasons:

- signing in, and second-factor codes,
- captchas,
- checking something before it happens, such as a payment.

Then tell the agent, in your client, to continue.

## Do not work at the same moment

Nothing stops you and the agent from acting at once, and the result
confuses both. Wait for the agent's step to finish, or stop it first.

## Stop the agent

- **In your MCP client**: cancel the request. This is the normal way.
- **For certain**: choose **Stop without saving state**, in the menu beside
  **Sleep** on the session. A stopped session refuses agent calls until you
  choose **Start**. **Sleep** does not do this: a sleeping session wakes on
  the agent's next call.

A call that is already running ends within its time limit, 30 seconds by
default.
