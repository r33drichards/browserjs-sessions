# Copy and paste

Your computer's clipboard and the desktop's are separate. The **Clipboard**
box under the screen carries text between them. For files, see
[Move files in and out](/guides/files).

## Paste into the desktop

1. Choose **Send my clipboard**. Your browser may ask for permission to read
   the clipboard.
2. Click where the text should go on the desktop and press `Ctrl+V`.

If your browser does not allow that, paste the text into the box and choose
**Send to browser**, then press `Ctrl+V` on the desktop.

## Copy out of the desktop

1. Select text on the desktop and press `Ctrl+C`. The text appears in the
   Clipboard box.
2. Choose **Copy** to put it on your own clipboard.

The desktop runs Linux, so its shortcuts use `Ctrl`, also when you are on a
Mac.
