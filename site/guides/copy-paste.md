# Copy and paste

Your computer's clipboard and the session's are separate. The **Clipboard**
box under the screen carries text between them. It carries text only; for
anything else see [Move files in and out](/guides/files).

## Paste into the session

1. Choose **Send my clipboard**. Your browser may ask for permission to read
   the clipboard.
2. Click where the text should go in the session's browser and press
   `Ctrl+V`.

If your browser does not allow that, paste the text into the box and choose
**Send to browser**, then press `Ctrl+V` in the session.

## Copy out of the session

1. Select text in the session's browser and press `Ctrl+C`. The text appears
   in the Clipboard box.
2. Choose **Copy** to put it on your own clipboard.

The session runs Linux, so its shortcuts use `Ctrl`, also on a Mac.
