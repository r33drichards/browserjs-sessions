# Introducing browserjs sessions

*1 October 2026*

Agents are good at using a browser, until the browser forgets who they are.
A fresh headless browser per task means signing in again every time, and a
task you cannot watch.

browserjs sessions is our answer. A session is one Chromium in the cloud that
is yours and stays:

- **A live view.** The browser is on your screen, in the page. You can click
  and type in it, and see what an agent does.
- **An MCP endpoint.** Every session has its own. Add it to Claude or any
  other MCP client as a connector, and the agent can drive the browser.
- **A disk.** Logins and tabs survive. Sign in once, in the live view, and
  the agent finds the browser signed in.
- **Sleep and wake.** A session that is not used goes to sleep. The next
  call wakes it, with its pages as they were.

There is also a clipboard box, a way to move files in and out, full screen,
and a browser that follows the size of your window.

## Try it

Sign in at [app.browserjs.com](https://app.browserjs.com) with Google or
GitHub, choose **New session**, and follow
[Your first session](/tutorials/first-session).

## What comes next

We are working on session URLs by path, policies for what an agent may do in
a session, and a Terraform provider. None of these is available yet.
